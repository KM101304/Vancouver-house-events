Take the data

The listings are a static, versioned, CORS-open feed. No key, no rate limit, no account. Build something with it.

  • events.json Everything upcoming, normalized to one schema.
  • events.ics Subscribe in Google or Apple Calendar and the city fills itself in.
  • venues.json Rooms currently programming, and what they book.
  • meta.json Last refresh time and per-source health.
